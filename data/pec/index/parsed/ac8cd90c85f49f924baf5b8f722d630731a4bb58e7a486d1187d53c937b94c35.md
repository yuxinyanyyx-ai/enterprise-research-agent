Asset overview
3
Drug candidate
BI 3802876 is a bispecific molecule binding FAP and TRAILR2.
Clinical hypothesis
Selectively removing FAP+ TRAILR2+ activated Hepatic Stellate Cells will halt/reverse liver fibrosis
Unique MOA
Directly targeting the source of liver fibrosis
FAP binding provides cell specificity and clusters TRAILR2 mediates cell killing
Indication
MASH cF4 is a sentinel population with the potential to expand to other etiologies as a Pan-Cirrhotic Therapy
Treatment paradigm
Intravenous drug, monthly or longer
Short term/Cycling/Induction therapy
Competition
No direct clinical competition for MoA in MASH
Pre-clinical results
Demonstrated anti-fibrotic PoC in LiverChip (in vitro) fibrosis model with clinically relevant biomarkers (see next slide)
Phase I results
BI 3802876 may cause small increases in hs-cTnT, which are of uncertain clinical significance at this time.
