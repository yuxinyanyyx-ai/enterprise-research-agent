## Slide 15

Evidence: chunk-d33e7f4209ca

Placebo group allowed only 20 mg/d steroid treatment in first 26 weeks, which is often insufficient to control PG progression. This may reduce patient enrollment intention.
Mild PG cases treated with topical or low-dose oral JAK inhibitors may achieve complete remission and thus might not require enrollment in this clinical trial. The patients enrolled are typically more complex cases, making it important to: 1) evaluate the therapeutic efficacy of Spesolimab in this population, and 2) consider that placebo allocation could pose significant challenges for these patients who often require active treatment.
Peking University Third Hospital’s treatment goal for PG is “onset of effect in 1 week and healing in 8 weeks.” The primary endpoint of this clinical trial is assessed at week 26, which is later than the hospital’s treatment goal and may lead to a decreased confidence in Spesolimab efficacy.
In the inclusion and exclusion criteria, the requirement for the target ulcer area of PG to be 5-80 cm² has a relatively narrow range, which may exclude some patients.
Peking University Third Hospital adopts a “ 0 Corticosteroid ” treatment regimen for PG, generally using JAK inhibitors, which show efficacy within one week, with improvement of skin lesions and pain. If the effect is not satisfactory, adalimumab is used, which can achieve favorable outcomes.
Wound care is essential for all PG patients, and the timing of debridement and skin grafting is crucial. Debridement and skin grafting are usually performed during the non-active phase to promote ulcer healing; if in the active phase and the ulcer site has poor growth of new tissue, debridement can also be cautiously implemented, which can also help ulcer recovery.
PG clinical trial related insights from Chinese PI (Prof. Wenhui WANG)
These insights have already be shared with the global evidence lead of Spesolimab(Thoma,Dr.,Christian), the clinical project lead for PG ( Elgadi, Dr. Mabrouk), Medical advisor of TA Med Affairs (Semeco,Dr.,Juan)
