## Slide 3

Evidence: chunk-82de524e3455

Study background
The cardiovascular and kidney benefits of SGLT2 inhibitors in patients with chronic kidney disease (CKD) are well established in large pivotal trials. This has prompted updates to SGLT2 inhibitor drug licences and the publication/update of clinical guidelines relating to their use, including ‘Kidney Disease: Improving Global Outcomes’ (KDIGO) guidelines in 2024 and ‘Guidelines for the early evaluation and management of chronic kidney disease in China’ in 2023.
However, the implementation of updated SGLT2 inhibitor guidelines and prescribing in the real-world CKD population remains largely unknown
Understanding treatment opportunity gaps, highlighting potential barriers and disparities are essential in guiding the successful GDMT.
Kidney Disease: Improving Global Outcomes (KDIGO). Kidney Int 2024;105:S117.

## Slide 4

Evidence: chunk-73970c2ac6e9

The Gap Between SGLT2i Guideline Recommendations and Real-World Practice in CKD Management Highlights the Urgent Need for Real-World Research
Established Cardiorenal Benefits of SGLT2i
Large clinical trials have demonstrated that SGLT2 inhibitors (SGLT2i) significantly reduce cardiovascular and renal events in CKD patients, leading to their inclusion as first-line therapy in both global (KDIGO 2024) and Chinese (2023/2025) guidelines.
Discrepancy Between Guidelines and Clinical Practice
Despite strong recommendations, the real-world prescription rate, treatment patterns, and influencing factors for SGLT2i use among CKD patients in China remain unclear. There is a lack of systematic, nationwide data, which hinders effective guideline implementation and maximization of patient benefit.
Rationale for the Study
There is an urgent need for real-world studies to systematically assess SGLT2i use, guideline adherence, and barriers in CKD management in China.
Understanding treatment opportunity gaps, highlighting potential barriers and disparities are essential in guiding the successful GDMT,  optimize CKD management strategies and improve patient outcomes.
