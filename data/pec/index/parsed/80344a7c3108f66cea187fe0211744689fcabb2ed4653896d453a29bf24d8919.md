Topic: China Integration Strategy into NR2B NAM MDD Ph3 | Background: The global asset team is proposing an US acceleration plan assuming a Go scenario from MDD Ph2 readouts. Two US focused trials will be conducted to support accelerated US launch by 4 months; and two global (RoW) trials will be conducted to support CN/JP NDA with postponed NDA and launch compared with the current plan (3 parallel MRCTs).

This approach aims to ensure simultaneously accelerated NDA submission, with registrational and operational feasibility for China without impacting the global timeline for the US launch. | TA-CEC
