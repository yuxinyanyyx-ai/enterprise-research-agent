Question: Treat time window: beyond 4.5 h from LKW based on the presence of ‘visible’ salvageable brain tissue | EMA Feedback (06 May 2025): The intention of the Applicant to evaluate the benefit-risk profile of TNK in AIS beyond the established 4.5 h treatment window is highly appreciated.

The TENACITY study introduces a new approach to decide eligibility for thrombolysis based on the presence of ‘visible’ salvageable brain tissue, instead of time from onset of AIS as proxy of the presence of salvageable brain tissue. If confirmed, this appears a paradigm shift in the treatment of AIS | CDE Feedback (24 Apr 2025): The available study data do not support the unrestricted extension of the thrombolytic time window. Furnish a robust foundation for the rational establishment of an appropriate thrombolytic time window.

发病4.5小时以内与发病时间超过4.5小时的急性缺血性卒中患者的获益-风险特征可能存在差异，通常情况下，超窗时间越长，溶栓治疗的获益可能更小、出血风险可能更高，应审慎、逐步的延长溶栓治疗时间窗。
申请人提交了针对超4.5小时溶栓时间窗的IIT研究文献资料，未提交注册临床试验数据，建议必要时可考虑开展探索性临床试验，探索不同剂量、不同时间窗的安全有效性特征趋势。
另外，现有研究资料不支持不限制时间窗，建议对已完成的IIT研究文献数据进行充分分析，可对比多个不同溶栓时间窗的安全性和有效性，为合理拟定适宜的溶栓时间窗提供充分依据。 | Mingfule CTA: 4.5-24h
