## Slide 3

Evidence: chunk-7faf957c8a5a

Background
Nerandomilast has launched in China in 2026, providing a novel therapeutic option for patients with IPF and PPF
While clinical trials have established its efficacy and safety, patients' real‑world treatment experiences remain unknown, including treatment initiation, adherence, and daily life impact
Understanding real‑world patient experience is critical to inform future clinical practice, including treatment decision‑making and long‑term management strategies
Insights from physicians will support optimized patient education and long-term management
Data from study 1199-0562 on unmet needs in IPF patients provides a critical foundation for this qualitative study
Key findings from
1199-0562
This study highlights that patients’ unmet needs and treatment expectations extend beyond symptom relief and disease control, including hopes for fewer side effects, improved tolerability, and greater affordability.
